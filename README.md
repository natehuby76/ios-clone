# StarPilot Galaxy for iOS

Open Galaxy on your iPhone to manage settings on your comma. Share a destination from Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze to send a route to your comma.

**Getting started?** Park, connect your iPhone and comma to the same Wi-Fi, and follow the [iPhone setup guide](QUICK-START.md) to open Galaxy and pair Bluetooth.
